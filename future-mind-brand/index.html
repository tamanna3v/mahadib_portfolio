Section 01

The Logo

01

Our logo pairs the Spark mark — an outlined lightning bolt inside a solid Future Purple square — with the wordmark. The bolt stands for the moment of insight we bring to clients: quick, clear thinking that turns into action. Always use the supplied artwork; never redraw or retype it.

Clear space

X X X X

Keep a margin of X on every side, where X = ½ the width of the mark. No text, image or edge may enter this space.

Minimum size

Lockup
35 mm / 140 px wide
Mark only
8 mm / 24 px

Below these sizes the bolt outline becomes unreadable. Use the mark alone for favicons, app icons and social avatars.

Colour versions

Primary — on white or light backgrounds
On Mind Mint — brand panels, covers
Reversed — on Future Purple or dark photos
Mono — single-colour print, stamps, fax

Don’t

Rotate or tilt the mark
Recolour with off-brand colours
Stretch or squash
Add outlines, borders or shadows
