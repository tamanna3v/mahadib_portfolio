The Process

Four steps, four owners, one outcome.

Each step has a clear owner and a clear output — nobody moves to the next step until the current one is done.

In Detail

What each step actually means.

Step 1

First Meeting — Program Team Leads

  • Program Team always leads the first client meeting — for SLDP, AIPA, RL, or any programme.
  • Program Team clearly explains the programme's content, intent, outcome, and approach — connecting it to the client's specific context, not just presenting it.
  • Sales and Communication provide coordination and support; the core thinking and explanation must come from the Program Team.
Step 2

Detailing & Requirement Understanding

  • If more detail is needed, hold an in-person or online session with the Head of Training / Head of HR, involving CPO/CHRO-level stakeholders where relevant.
  • Goal: understand the actual requirement, business/problem context, participant profile, expected outcome and success criteria, and how our programme addresses it.
Step 3

Quote & Proposal

  • Send a proper quote and proposal only after requirements are understood.
  • The proposal covers recommendation, rationale, scope, and expected outcome — not price alone — and explains why this approach fits the client.
  • The quote reflects our recommendation; it is never a mechanical quotation of only what the client asked for.
Step 4

Follow-up & Lock

  • Use follow-up communication to progressively lock scope, dates, participant numbers, and other requirements.
  • Confirm final scope, commercial terms, and delivery expectations clearly.
  • Hand over cleanly to the implementation/delivery team so nothing is missed.
⛔ Strictly Not To Do

No one on the team acts as a "postman" — passing information along is not enough. Business, Operations, Sales, and Communication must each carry at least 80–90% of the Program Lead's level of understanding, contributing actively with business understanding, operational input, sales context, and communication insight.

Core Principle

Understand → Diagnose → Recommend → Quote → Lock.

Every AIPA presales conversation follows this chain — skipping a link is how deals go wrong.
