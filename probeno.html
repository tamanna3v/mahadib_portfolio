The Gap

AI is racing ahead.
Most classrooms are not.

AI is reshaping how people learn and work — but the teachers who shape the next generation were never trained for it. Left unaddressed, this widens an already deep digital divide: students with connected, confident mentors surge ahead, while everyone else falls further behind.

  • ! Teachers lack structured, trustworthy AI upskilling built for the classroom.
  • ! Fear, hype and misinformation crowd out responsible, ethical use.
  • ! Access to AI tools and know-how is unevenly distributed — a growing divide.
  • ! One-off student workshops don't scale; the leverage point is the educator.

The Standard We Build On

Aligned to the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers.

Rather than reinvent the wheel, Probeno is structured around UNESCO's globally-recognised framework — five competency dimensions, each progressing through three levels of mastery. It gives teachers a credible, portable credential and gives sponsors confidence in a proven standard.

📘 UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers · 5 Dimensions × 3 Levels

The Five Competency Dimensions

💛

Human-Centred Mindset

Keeping people, agency and social good at the centre of every AI decision in education.

⚖️

Ethics of AI

Privacy, fairness, transparency and accountability — the guardrails for classroom use.

🧠

AI Foundations & Applications

How AI actually works, what it can and can't do, and where it fits in learning.

📚

AI Pedagogy

Designing lessons, assessment and feedback that use AI to deepen — not replace — learning.

📈

AI for Professional Learning

Teachers using AI for their own growth, planning and lifelong development.

The Three Progression Levels

Level 1

Acquire

Build foundational awareness and confidence — understand the tools and the risks.

Level 2

Deepen

Apply AI meaningfully in real teaching, assessment and classroom practice.

Level 3

Create

Innovate, adapt and lead — mentoring peers and shaping AI-ready school culture.

The Curriculum

A block-by-block pathway, ready to run.

Six practical modules take a teacher from first contact with AI to confidently leading in their school — each grounded in a UNESCO competency dimension.

Module 1 · Foundations

Demystifying AI for the Classroom

What AI is, how generative tools work, and a clear-eyed view of capabilities and limits — no hype, no fear.

Dimension → AI Foundations & Applications
Module 2 · Ethics

Responsible & Ethical AI Use

Data privacy, bias, academic integrity and student safety — practical guardrails teachers can trust.

Dimension → Ethics of AI · Human-Centred Mindset
Module 3 · Pedagogy

AI-Powered Lesson & Assessment Design

Build lesson plans, differentiated materials, rubrics and feedback loops that deepen learning.

Dimension → AI Pedagogy
Module 4 · Practice

Tools in the Teacher's Workflow

Hands-on with AI assistants for planning, grading support, and everyday administrative time-savers.

Dimension → AI for Professional Learning
Module 5 · Students

Guiding Students to Use AI Well

Teaching AI literacy, critical thinking and integrity to students — the ripple made real.

Dimension → AI Pedagogy · Ethics of AI
Module 6 · Leadership

Becoming an AI Champion

Capstone project, certification, and a peer-mentoring plan to spread capability across the school.

Level → Create · Multiply the Impact
